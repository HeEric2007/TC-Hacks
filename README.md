# TC Hacks

The website for **TC Hacks**, a student organization at the University of Minnesota that runs the Twin Cities' student hackathon, plus networking nights, resume reviews and build nights throughout the year.

Our flagship event is a free 24-hour hackathon on the UMN Twin Cities campus, where teams of up to four build a project from scratch, attend workshops, work with mentors and present to judges from Minnesota companies. It's open to college students from any school and any major, and no prior experience is needed.

Between hackathons, we host build nights, workshops, tech talks, speed networking, resume reviews, interview prep, company visits and demo nights, all free for students thanks to our partners.
