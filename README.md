# TC Hacks

The website for **TC Hacks**, a student organization at the University of Minnesota that runs the Twin Cities' student hackathon, plus networking nights, resume reviews and build nights throughout the year.

## Pages

- **Home**: what TC Hacks is, quick facts about the hackathon, what we do, and a sign-up form for updates.
- **About**: our mission and the team.
- **Events**: the annual 24-hour hackathon, plus recurring events like build nights, workshops, tech talks, speed networking, resume reviews, interview prep, company visits and demo night.
- **Sponsor**: how companies can get involved, from prize tracks and workshops to mentoring, judging and on-site recruiting.
- **FAQ**: answers for students thinking about coming.

## Look and feel

A clean, flat design built around an illustrated Minneapolis skyline, with smooth page transitions and sections that fade in as you scroll.

Built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS and Motion.
